Privacy

How we handle your details.

Last updated 9 November 2026. This notice covers the pre-order form on vh.do. It's written plainly on purpose. If anything is unclear, email us.

Who we are

v/h is run by Vedh Krishnan, operating as v/h. We decide how your details are used. Contact: vedh@vh.do.

What we keep

When you reserve a lamp, we keep your email address, the price range and size you picked, the currency you saw, the country your request came from (worked out by our hosting provider, not from your IP address, which we don't store), and when you signed up.

Why

To hold your place for the lamp, to email you about your pre-order and about v/h, and to understand what price and size people want. We do this because you asked us to when you filled in the form. You can withdraw that at any time.

Where it's kept

Your details are stored with Cloudflare, our hosting provider, in data centres in the European Union. Cloudflare processes them for us under its data processing terms. We don't sell your details, rent them or share them with advertisers.

How long

Until 12 months after the lamp goes on sale, or sooner if you ask us to delete them. Then we delete them.

Your choices

You can ask to see your details, correct them or delete them, and you can unsubscribe from our emails at any time. Email vedh@vh.do and we'll do it within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK (ico.org.uk) or to the data protection authority where you live.

Cookies

The pre-order page doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking.
